Fort Jesus Museum

KES 1,200

Non-Resident Entry

location_on Nkurumah Rd, Old Town • 2.4 km away • wb_sunny 29°C Sea Breeze

Built by the Portuguese between 1593 and 1596 according to designs by Giovanni Battista Cairati, this monumental bastion guarded the Old Port of Mombasa. Wander through thick coral-rag battlements, cannons overlooking the sea passage, and an Omani audience hall displaying intricate Swahili and Islamic woodwork.
