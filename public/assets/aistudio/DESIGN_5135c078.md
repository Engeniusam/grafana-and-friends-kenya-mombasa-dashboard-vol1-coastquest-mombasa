## Brand & Style

This design system manifests the vibrancy of the Kenyan Swahili coast merged with modern high-performance travel engineering. The aesthetic bridges the exploratory utility of Google Travel, the warm storytelling of Airbnb Experiences, and the social validation of TripAdvisor, refined through an Indian Ocean vernacular.

### Personality & Atmosphere
- **Maritime Horizon:** Crisp, oceanic, luminous, and expansive.
- **Swahili Craft Modernism:** Earthy sandstone warmth intertwined with deep cobalt waters, coral reefs, and carved geometric architecture.
- **High-Velocity Travel Tech:** Hyper-functional data density, real-time observability indicators, and telemetry-inspired micro-details designed for modern field-readiness.

### Design Movements
- **Frosted Maritime Glassmorphism:** Translucent surface cards floating over subtle ocean gradients and vivid photographic textures.
- **Modern Coastal Minimalism:** Generous whitespace, razor-sharp typographic hierarchy, high-contrast badges, and tactile rounded surfaces.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the transition of Fort Jesus coral rag stone to the Indian Ocean shelf at Diani and Mombasa Old Town.

- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Deep Swahili Navy):** Serves as structural anchor for high-contrast typography, primary navigation shells, and dominant interactive buttons.
- **Secondary (`#0EA5E9` - Indian Ocean Blue):** Represents active interactive states, links, map vectors, and booking flows.
- **Tertiary (`#14B8A6` - Wasini Marine Turquoise):** Directs the eye to eco-certifications, marine safaris, verification indicators, and verified experience checkmarks.
- **Accent Coral (`#FB7185` - Malindi Coral):** Reserved strictly for high-urgency call-to-actions, favorites/wishlist toggles, instant-booking indicators, and system alerts.
- **Accent Sunset Amber (`#F59E0B` - Likoni Sunset):** Used for review star ratings, historical landmark markers, and sunset cruise highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` - Sand White):** Emulates sun-bleached coastal sand, creating a clean backdrop that prevents visual fatigue.
- **Surface Elevation Layers:**
  - Base canvas: `#F8FAFC`
  - Frosted Card Surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
  - Border Hairline: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`
  - Elevated Popover Surface: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to ensure geometric balance, open counters, and high legibility across discovery feeds and dense reservation checkout tables.

### Rules & Formatting
- **Display & Headings:** Tightly tracked headings (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) maintain an authoritative editorial presence reminiscent of luxury travel glossies.
- **Telemetry & Metadata (`label-sm`):** Rendered uppercase with generous tracking (`0.06em`) for coordinate tags, live occupancy trackers, Grafana Faro observability metrics, and marine tide charts.
- **Numbers & Prices:** Numeric displays in listing cards use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) with `fontWeight: 700` for rapid scannability across currency variations (KES, USD, EUR).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt spatial cadence with an adaptive fluid 12-column grid.

### Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, max-width bounded at `1440px`. Split-screen view option (55% interactive map, 45% listing stream).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Sticky category bar with horizontal swipe overflow.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-sm: 1rem`. Full-width discovery feed with edge-to-edge media cards.

### Layout Principles
- Use `space-md` for standard interior padding in cards and modal modules.
- Use `space-lg` to separate decoupled discovery widgets (e.g., dhow excursions vs. Old Town culinary walking tours).
- Preserve `margin` clearance around search bar docks and bottom sticky action drawers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines physical light reflection with frosted oceanic atmospheric depth.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Sand base `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, structural container boundaries formed by `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Level 1 (Listing & Experience Cards):** Surface `#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with blur `12px`. Drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Docks):** Drop shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.08)`. Slight ocean blue ambient tint.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Flight/Booking Bar & Filtering Flyouts):** Drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Full Lightbox Galleries):** Drop shadow: `0 32px 64px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.24)`. Accompanied by a backdrop scrim: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a roundedness factor of `2` (base `0.5rem` / `8px` up to `1rem` / `16px` for primary elements). This mirrors smoothed ocean sea-glass and modern automotive hardware design.

### Corner Radii Mappings
- **Cards & Experience Containers:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`).
- **Interactive Buttons & Input Fields:** `rounded-lg` (`0.75rem` to `1rem` / `12px` to `16px`).
- **Pill Badges, Chips & Meta Indicators:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to contrast against rectangular glass panels.
- **Modal Sheets & Drawer Bottom Panels:** Top corners `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`), bottom corners flat (`0px`) on mobile viewports.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.75rem`. Hover state transitions to `#1E293B` with `transform: translateY(-1px)`.
- **Secondary (Action):** Background `#0EA5E9`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used for checkout, booking validation, and real-time boat charter confirmations.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Background `transparent`, border `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`, text `#0F172A`. Subtle turquoise glow on focus.
- **Wishlist Action Button:** Circular floating glass button with Malindi Coral heart toggle (`#FB7185`), `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Default: Height `36px`, sand background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, border `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, text `#0F172A`.
- Active: Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-color `#0F172A`. Includes an optional badge counter in Wasini Marine Turquoise (`#14B8A6`).

### Input Fields & Search Shell
- Floating glassmorphic multi-segment search hub (Destination / Dates / Travelers).
- Height: `56px`. Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- Focus State: Inner glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.25)` and border `#0EA5E9`.

### Cards (Experiences & Stays)
- Vertical layout featuring a 4:3 image carousel with border radius `0.75rem` inside a `1rem` padded glass frame.
- Subtle badge overlay at top left for experience status (e.g., "Verified Swahili Host", "Likoni Sunset Sail").
- Price lockup at bottom right using `tabular-nums`, bold typography, and secondary duration copy ("per person", "per day").

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkbox: `20px × 20px`, `rounded` (`4px`). Unchecked has `1.5px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.2)`. Checked is `#0EA5E9` with a crisp white check vector.
- Radio: Concentric circle with `#0EA5E9` filled center on active.

### Observability & Telemetry Badges (Grafana Faro & Tech Accents)
- **Live Health & Tide Dot:** `8px` pulsating green (`#10B981`) or turquoise (`#14B8A6`) circular dot denoting live sea conditions or high-confidence host latency.
- **Performance Badge:** Monospaced coordinate tag (`4.0435° S, 39.6682° E`) in `label-sm` font for marine points of interest.
